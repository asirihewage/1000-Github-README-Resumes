# Kamil Adamczyk

I'm a Tech Lead living in Montevideo, Uruguay.

## The beginning

My story started with a broken computer and a library book about
Mocha. I wrote my first program to make a turtle draw a square —
and I was hooked.

## The middle

Years later I've shipped everything from schema-migrator to flashcard-app, taught
myself Ansible on nights and weekends, and learned that the best
code is the code your future self can read at 2 AM.

## The now

Today I spend my time building qr-scanner and helping other developers
grow. I believe in boring technology, clear commit messages, and
good documentation.

## The next

Next stop: Swift at scale, a few long walks, and whatever the
internet surprises me with next.
